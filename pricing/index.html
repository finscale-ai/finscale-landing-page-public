FAQs

What is FinScale's Agentic AI and how does it work?

FinScale uses Agentic AI a highly autonomous system to manage and automate complex financial tasks. It constantly categorizes transactions, reconciles accounts, tracks spending in real-time, and flags anomalies, delivering accurate books without manual effort.

How does FinScale compare to traditional accounting software like Xero or QuickBooks?

FinScale automates bookkeeping end-to-end, keeping your books accurate, up-to-date, and audit-ready so you can focus on growing your business, not managing transactions.

Which integrations are supported by FinScale?

We offer unlimited integrations with all major platforms crucial for SMBs, including accounting software like QuickBooks and Xero, and financial platforms like Stripe and Plaid.
